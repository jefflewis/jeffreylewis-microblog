About

Engineering depth without the ceremony.

I’m Jeff Lewis, a staff mobile engineer with more than a decade of experience across native mobile, web, and backend systems. My strongest work sits where product requirements meet difficult technical constraints: intermittent connectivity, long-lived state, evolving workflows, and software that must remain understandable as teams and products grow.

My background spans field service, retail, commerce, and client applications. I bring hands-on implementation, architecture judgment, and a bias toward straightforward systems that hold up in production.
